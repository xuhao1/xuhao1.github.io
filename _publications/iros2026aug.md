**Honorable Mention, IROS 2026 Best Paper Award on Safety, Security, and Rescue Robotics.**

AUG is a perception-aware UAV planning framework for GNSS-denied, feature-sparse environments. It combines topology-guided global routing, robocentric local trajectory optimization, and yaw planning that keeps informative LiDAR measurements in view for more stable odometry.
