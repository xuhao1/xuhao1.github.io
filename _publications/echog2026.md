ECHO-G lets humanoid robots gesture while they talk. It jointly uses text and speech latents to generate semantically rich full-body motion that lands on the beat, runs end-to-end whole-body control, and fits on an embedded Jetson Orin AGX. Joint work with Mondo Robotics and the HKUST Aerial Robotics Group.
